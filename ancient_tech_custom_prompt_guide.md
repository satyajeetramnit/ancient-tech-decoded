**Custom Prompt Guide for Writing Episodes – Ancient Tech Decoded**

Use the following copy-ready prompts to generate each section of your episode in GPT-4.5 (RESEARCH PREVIEW) or GPT-4 Turbo for best quality.

---

### ✦ Section 1: The Storytelling Hook

```
Using the following episode outline:

Episode Title: “[INSERT TITLE]”  
Scene: [Describe vivid mythological moment]  
Hook: [Insert poetic, speculative teaser question]

Please write only **Section 1: The Storytelling Hook**.

Requirements:
- Write in the voice of a **timeless, poetic narrator**
- Use **immersive storytelling**: cinematic imagery, tension, and mood
- Tone: **Mythic, prophetic, ancient-yet-relevant**
- DO NOT explain technology yet — this is a *prologue-style scene* to draw the audience in
- End with a **haunting or poetic speculative question** that acts as a soft transition to Section 2
```

---

### ✦ Section 2: Mythological Reference & Context

```
Now continue with **Section 2: Mythological Reference & Context**.

Requirements:
- Clearly recount the myth or epic reference from [Insert Scripture], without sounding like a textbook
- Keep the tone narrative and reverent, not academic
- You may quote or paraphrase Sanskrit verses where appropriate, but frame them poetically
- Maintain immersion, as if the narrator is unveiling forgotten truths
- Transition gently toward the decoding (to be done in Section 3)
```

---

### ✦ Section 3: Speculative Science/Tech Interpretation

```
Now generate **Section 3: Speculative Science/Tech Interpretation**.

Requirements:
- Re-express the myth in speculative scientific terms
- Imagine what modern or future tech could match the described effects
- DO NOT be afraid to draw bold or imaginative parallels (e.g., nuclear energy, AI, directed plasma)
- Tone: Analytical but poetic — grounded in myth-tech synthesis, not dry theory
- End the section with a bridge into real-world comparisons for Section 4
```

---

### ✦ Section 4: Real-World Modern Parallels

```
Now write **Section 4: Real-World Modern Parallels**.

Requirements:
- Compare the mythical technology to current or emerging scientific equivalents
- Examples may include: EMPs, bioweapons, quantum computing, nuclear fallout, etc.
- Make these parallels vivid and unsettling, with examples or historical echoes (e.g., Hiroshima)
- Maintain mythic reverence while drawing real-world tension
- End with a transition or open question inviting deeper reflection
```

---

### ✦ Section 5: Philosophical or Spiritual Insight

```
Now generate **Section 5: Philosophical or Spiritual Insight**.

Requirements:
- Zoom out to reflect on ethical, karmic, or spiritual implications of the tech/myth being discussed
- Consider dharma, karma, spiritual maturity, misuse of power, and cosmic law
- Use thoughtful, poetic language
- Pose deep philosophical questions without giving clear answers
```

---

### ✦ Section 6: Poetic “What If?” Closing Reflection

```
Now write **Section 6: Poetic ‘What If?’ Closing Reflection**.

Requirements:
- End the episode with a lyrical, poetic meditation
- Include 2–3 speculative “what if” questions
- Let the tone be both mysterious and reflective, like an ancient voice whispering through time
- Keep it brief (2–4 paragraphs max), powerful, and moving

> Format example:
> What if... [first speculative idea]?
> What if... [second]?
> And what if... [final poetic closing thought]?
```

---

Use this guide per section to generate rich, immersive, and narratively consistent episodes for your *Ancient Tech Decoded* series.
