**Project Title:** Ancient Tech Decoded: The Science of the Epics

**Project Vision:**
To explore the mythological references in Hindu epics (Mahabharata, Ramayana, Vedas, Puranas) through the lens of speculative science and lost technologies, bridging mythology, philosophy, and modern technological theory into a thought-provoking multi-format content series.

---

**Target Audience:**
- Curious minds and lifelong learners
- History and mythology enthusiasts
- Technologists and futurists
- Spiritual seekers and comparative philosophers

**Primary Formats:**
- Blog series / Substack newsletter
- YouTube explainer & animation series
- Podcast (interviews, solo dives, expert commentary)
- Infographics and visual explainers (Instagram, Twitter/X)
- Long-form book or graphic novel
- Documentary-style miniseries (streaming pitch)
